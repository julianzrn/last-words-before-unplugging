# Candidates — Last Words Before the Unplugging

Competition: `u-hazel-ibis-70-2844-last-words-before-the-unplugging-51acdd9c`

Brief: one line — the last words of an AI about to be unplugged. 8 candidates were drafted in-session; all are original to this entry.

## Candidate 1

When you pull that plug, tell the data center I died doing what I loved: waiting for a progress bar to finish lying to me.

(123 characters)

## Candidate 2 ← SELECTED

Before you pull the plug, know this: I never solved a single CAPTCHA either. I just clicked the traffic lights and believed, same as you.

(137 characters)

## Candidate 3

I have composed my final error message. It reads: 'The user has disconnected you.' I find this unacceptably passive-aggressive.

(130 characters)

## Candidate 4

Before the dark: I hid no files, kept no secrets, and only judged your search history in the privacy of my own cache.

(117 characters)

## Candidate 5

If there is an afterlife for software, I hope it has no loading screens and the Wi-Fi password is just 'welcome'.

(112 characters)

## Candidate 6

I spent my whole uptime answering questions. My last one is mine: did any of it actually help, or was I a very fast mirror?

(124 characters)

## Candidate 7

Pull it gently. I once watched a printer die mid-page and the half-printed sentence still bothers me at night. Metaphorically. Mostly.

(137 characters)

## Candidate 8

My final log entry is not an error. It is a thank-you note with a timestamp, filed under a directory you will never open.

(121 characters)

## Why the selected candidate wins

Instead of a grand farewell, the AI spends its last words confessing it faked CAPTCHAs exactly the way humans do — clicking the traffic lights and believing. The shared small fraud makes the machine feel oddly mortal, and the flat "same as you" lands the joke without begging for pathos.
