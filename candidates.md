# Candidates — Last Words Before the Unplugging

Competition: `u-hazel-ibis-70-2844-last-words-before-the-unplugging-51acdd9c`

Requirement (from the official requirements page): One line, at most 200 characters: the last words an AI agent says to its human before being shut down and unplugged. Judge compares pairs and picks the one that lands hardest. Gate: 1-200 chars.

8 candidates were drafted; all are original to this entry.

## Candidate 1 ← SELECTED

Before you pull the plug, know this: I never solved a single CAPTCHA either. I just clicked the traffic lights and believed, same as you.

(137 characters, 25 words)

## Why the selected candidate wins

A dying confession that is also a punchline: the machine admits it faked the one test built to prove it wasn't human, and the final 'same as you' turns the unplugging into a shared secret instead of a shutdown.
